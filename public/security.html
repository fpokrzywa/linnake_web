Security

Where your data lives

Your fine-tuning data stays on your hardware by default, or in the environment we agree on.

We fine-tune an open-weight model on that data, on your own hardware first. A hosted GPU is an option if you need it.

Your data never fine-tunes anyone else's model.

Who can access it

The setup is designed to limit access to the people authorized for your deployment. We define who can access what with you before work starts.

What goes live

Every new version has to pass a quality check before it goes live. If it fails, the current version stays.
